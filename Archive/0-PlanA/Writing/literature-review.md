## Working Opening

The rapid diffusion of generative AI is changing the conditions under which employee performance is produced and evaluated. In traditional performance appraisal systems, performance outcomes are commonly treated as signals of an individual's competence, effort, and judgment. However, when employees produce work through repeated interaction with AI systems, the resulting output may reflect a hybrid contribution that is difficult to decompose into human and machine components. This raises a core HRM question: can a performance appraisal system remain procedurally fair when the source of performance contribution is ambiguous?
