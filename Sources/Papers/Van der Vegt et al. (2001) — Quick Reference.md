
## 서지정보
Van der Vegt, G. S., Emans, B. J. M., & Van de Vliert, E. (2001). Patterns of interdependence in work teams: A two-level investigation of the relations with job and team satisfaction. *Personnel Psychology*, 54(1), 51–69.

## 본 연구에서의 역할
IV(인간-AI 협업 결합도) 측정 문항의 적응(adaptation) 근거
→ Task Interdependence 척도를 인간-AI 맥락으로 수정하여 사용

## 원문 Task Interdependence 5문항 (p.58)
1. "I have to obtain information and advice from my colleagues in order to complete my work"
2. "I depend on my colleagues for the completion of my work"
3. "I have a one-person job; I rarely have to check or work with others" *(역문항)*
4. "I have to work closely with my colleagues to do my work properly"
5. "In order to complete their work, my colleagues have to obtain information and advice from me"

척도: 5점 리커트 (1 = strongly agree, 5 = strongly disagree)
Cronbach's α = .76

## 인간-AI 맥락 적응 검토
| 번호 | 적응 가능성 | 수정 방향 |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| 1번 | ✅ 높음 | "colleagues" → "AI" |
| 2번 | ✅ 높음 | "colleagues" → "AI" |
| 3번 | ⚠️ 주의 | 역문항 유지, 표현 다듬기 필요 |
| 4번 | ✅ 높음 | "colleagues" → "AI" |
| 5번 | ❌ 낮음 | 삭제 또는 완전 재작성 필요 (방향 반전 문제) |
