# Morgeson & Humphrey (2006) — Quick Reference

## 서지정보
Morgeson, F. P., & Humphrey, S. E. (2006). The Work Design Questionnaire (WDQ): Developing and validating a comprehensive measure for assessing job design and the nature of work. *Journal of Applied Psychology*, 91(6), 1321–1339.

## 본 연구에서의 역할
IV(인간-AI 협업 결합도) 측정 문항의 적응(adaptation) 근거
→ WDQ의 Received Interdependence 척도를 인간-AI 맥락으로 수정하여 사용
→ Van der Vegt(2001)과 함께 IV 문항의 이중 출처 정당화

## 핵심 구성개념 — Interdependence (p.1338 Appendix)

### Initiated Interdependence (3문항)
1. "The job requires me to accomplish my job before others complete their job"
2. "Other jobs depend directly on my job"
3. "Unless my job gets done, other jobs cannot be completed"

### Received Interdependence (3문항) ← 본 연구 핵심
1. "The job activities are greatly affected by the work of other people"
2. "The job depends on the work of many different people for its completion"
3. "My job cannot be done unless others do their work"

척도: 5점 리커트 (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree)
Received α = .84, Initiated α = .80

## 인간-AI 맥락 적응 검토
| 문항 | 적응 가능성 | 수정 방향 |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Initiated 1~3 | ⚠️ 낮음 | AI가 수혜자 위치 → 방향 반전 문제 |
| Received 1~3 | ✅ 높음 | "other people" → "AI" 로 적응 가능 |

## Orlikowski+Leonardi 연결
Received Interdependence 3번 — "My job cannot be done unless others do their work" — 이 Orlikowski(2007) 사회물질성 및 Leonardi(2011) 임브리케이션의 **구조적 얽힘** 개념과 직접 연결됨
